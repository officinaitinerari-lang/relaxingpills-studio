Fall asleep to planet Jupiter, the gentle giant of our Solar System — real science, softly told, for sleep, study or relaxation.

Tonight we drift slowly toward the largest world that circles the Sun. We follow the Babylonian astronomers who traced its path on clay, and Galileo, who saw its four moons in 1610. We float past the soft cloud bands of this gas giant, rest beside the Great Red Spot, an old storm about as wide as the Earth, and sink gently into its deep ocean of hydrogen.

Then we visit its family of moons: volcanic Io, icy Europa with its hidden ocean, Ganymede, the largest moon in the Solar System, and quiet Callisto. Everything about the Jupiter planet, told slowly: a calm journey with Pioneer, Voyager, Galileo and Juno, as the gentle giant keeps turning far away in the dark.
